# Interview guide (about 10 minutes, before showing the app)

Ask open questions and let them talk. Ask "Can you tell me about the last time…?" rather than "Would you…?". Write down literal quotes where you can.

## About them
1. What kind of business do you have, and how many people work there?
2. Who made your website, and who looks after it now?
3. Which phone do you use for work: Android or iPhone?

## How they keep an eye on things today
4. How do you find out if your website is not working? Tell me about the last time it happened.
5. How often do you look at your Google reviews? What do you do when you get a bad one?
6. Have you ever looked at how you show up on Google, or how fast your site is? With what?
7. Which apps or websites do you use for this today, if any? What do you like or dislike about them?

## Pain and value
8. What worries you most about your business online?
9. When would you want to hear about a problem: right away, once a week, or never?
10. If something is wrong, would you fix it yourself or ask someone? Who?

## Context
11. When and where would you look at an app like this: at the counter, at home, on the go?
12. Do you have good mobile reception in your shop?

Write down in `results.md`: the most important quote, and anything that surprised you.
