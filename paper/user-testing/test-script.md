# Test script (about 15 minutes)

Hand them the phone with the app open on the login screen. Read each task out loud. **Don't help or point**: if they're stuck for more than a minute, note it and move on.

Say first: "Please think out loud. Say what you're looking for and what you expect to happen."

| # | Task (read out loud) | Done when | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Log in with the demo account." | Home screen visible | Did they find the demo button? |
| 2 | "How is this business doing online, in your own words?" | They mention the score, or "needs work" and the things to fix | Did they understand the score? |
| 3 | "What should the owner fix first? Open it and tell me what to do." | Issue detail open, steps explained back | Was the explanation clear? Jargon? |
| 4 | "Say you've fixed it. Tell the app." | Pressed "Mark as fixed" | Did they expect the score to change right away? |
| 5 | "Is there a bad review without an answer? Deal with it." | Reviews screen, "Mark as replied" | Did they find the Reviews tab? |
| 6 | "Switch to the bicycle shop. What is wrong there?" | Settings, business switched, "website is down" | Did they find Settings? |
| 7 | (Optional) "Put this app on the home screen of the phone." | Installed, or iPhone instructions found | Android or iPhone? |

## Closing questions
1. What was the most useful thing you saw?
2. What was confusing or unnecessary?
3. Would you use this for your own business? How often?
4. What is missing for you to really use it?
5. On a scale of 1 to 5, how easy was it?
