# User testing kit

Chapter 25 asks you to test with at least three people from the target group and process their feedback. This kit covers one session of about 30 minutes per person.

## What you need
- The live app link on your phone, or theirs.
- This folder, printed or open on a laptop.
- A way to take notes. Don't record unless they agree (see `consent.md`).

## Steps per person
1. **Consent (2 min):** read `consent.md` out loud and ask for a yes.
2. **Interview (10 min):** ask the questions in `interview-guide.md` *before* showing the app, so their answers aren't influenced by it.
3. **Test (15 min):** give them the phone and read the tasks in `test-script.md` one by one. Don't help. Write down where they hesitate, what they say, and whether they finish.
4. **Wrap-up (3 min):** the closing questions at the bottom of `test-script.md`.
5. **Afterwards:** fill in one row per person in `results.md` the same day, while you still remember.

## After all three
- In `results.md`, list the problems that came up for two or more people, and the top 3 you will fix.
- Use the interview answers to fill in the ✏️ parts of `../PAPER.md`: where the problem statement comes from, the target group table, and the personas (`persona-template.md`).
- Call participants P1, P2 and P3 in everything you write. This repository is public, so no names, business names or contact details.

Who counts as the target group: someone who owns or runs a small local business with a website, such as a shop, practice, workshop or café. Family members count if they really run a business.
